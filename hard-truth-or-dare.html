Hard Truth or Dare Questions

Explore these questions and enjoy a fun game with your group. Choose a question, take turns and keep the game entertaining for everyone.

  1. What's one fear you've never told most people about?
  2. What's the hardest decision you've ever had to make?
  3. What's one mistake that taught you an important lesson?
  4. Have you ever apologized even though you didn't think you were completely wrong?
  5. What's one habit you'd genuinely like to change?
  6. What's something you've done that you're secretly proud of?
  7. What's one opinion you have that most of your friends disagree with?
  8. When was the last time you cried?
  9. What's one opportunity you regret not taking?
  10. What's something you wish you were better at?
  11. Have you ever judged someone and later realized you were wrong?
  12. What's one promise you've made to yourself?
  13. What's the biggest risk you've ever taken?
  14. What's one thing you would do differently if you could go back five years?
  15. What's something you've never had the courage to try?
  16. What's one truth about yourself that took you years to accept?
  17. Who has had the biggest positive influence on your life?
  18. What's one dream you're afraid might never happen?
  19. What's one thing you want people to remember about you?
  20. If you could receive one completely honest answer about your future, what would you ask?
  21. Give a completely honest compliment to every player.
  22. Tell the group about one goal you're currently working toward.
  23. Let another player choose a harmless challenge for you.
  24. Give a 60-second motivational speech without preparing.
  25. Tell a funny story from your life using dramatic acting.
  26. Sing a song without looking at the lyrics.
  27. Perform your best impression of yourself five years from now.
  28. Let the group choose a harmless word that you must use in your next three sentences.
  29. Give a one-minute presentation about something you know extremely well.
  30. Tell everyone one positive thing you genuinely appreciate about them.
  31. Act out your dream job for 30 seconds without speaking.
  32. Tell a story that includes three random objects chosen by the group.
  33. Try to make the group laugh using only facial expressions.
  34. Give an acceptance speech for an award you invent.
  35. Speak for one minute without using the words "yes" or "no."
  36. Recreate an embarrassing moment using only gestures.
  37. Invent a new game and explain its rules in 60 seconds.
  38. Give yourself a ridiculous superhero name and explain your superpower.
  39. Tell the group one thing you're determined to accomplish this year.
  40. End your turn by giving yourself a dramatic "mission accomplished" celebration.

How to Play

Take turns choosing a question and answer honestly or complete the challenge when appropriate. Keep the game respectful and comfortable for everyone.

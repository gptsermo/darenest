Holiday Truth or Dare Questions

Explore these questions and enjoy a fun game with your group. Choose a question, take turns and keep the game entertaining for everyone.

  1. What's your favorite holiday tradition?
  2. What's the best Christmas gift you've ever received?
  3. Have you ever secretly looked for your presents before Christmas?
  4. What's your favorite holiday food?
  5. Would you rather spend Christmas at home or travel somewhere new?
  6. What's your funniest holiday memory?
  7. What's your favorite Christmas movie?
  8. If you could spend Christmas anywhere in the world, where would you go?
  9. What's the best holiday party you've ever attended?
  10. Have you ever accidentally discovered what someone was getting you as a gift?
  11. What's your favorite holiday song?
  12. Would you rather decorate a huge Christmas tree or decorate an entire house?
  13. What's the most unusual gift you've ever received during the holidays?
  14. What's your favorite holiday dessert?
  15. If you could invite three fictional characters to your holiday dinner, who would they be?
  16. What's one holiday tradition you'd like to start?
  17. Have you ever forgotten to buy someone a holiday gift?
  18. What's your favorite thing about the holiday season?
  19. Would you rather have a snowy Christmas or a beach Christmas?
  20. What's one holiday memory you hope you'll never forget?
  21. Sing a Christmas song in your funniest voice.
  22. Pretend you're Santa delivering presents.
  23. Give a dramatic speech about your favorite holiday food.
  24. Pretend you're decorating the world's biggest Christmas tree.
  25. Create a ridiculous new holiday tradition.
  26. Act like you're a Christmas movie narrator.
  27. Give everyone a funny holiday nickname.
  28. Pretend you're Santa checking your naughty-or-nice list.
  29. Create a 20-second holiday commercial.
  30. Pretend you're opening the best gift you've ever received.
  31. Make up a holiday song using another player's name.
  32. Act like you're a holiday chef presenting dinner.
  33. Give a dramatic weather forecast for Christmas morning.
  34. Pretend you're a store employee during the busiest holiday shopping day.
  35. Create a funny holiday dance.
  36. Give a 30-second speech explaining why your favorite holiday dessert is the best.
  37. Pretend you're hosting a holiday TV special.
  38. Invent a new holiday and explain how people celebrate it.
  39. Give another player three genuine holiday compliments.

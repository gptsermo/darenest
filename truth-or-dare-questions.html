Popular Truth Questions

Use these as quick prompts when you want a conversation starter.

  1. Who in this group do you trust the most?
  2. What's the most embarrassing thing you've done in front of friends?
  3. What is one habit you wish you could change?
  4. What is the funniest prank you've ever played?
  5. Have you ever ignored a message on purpose?
  6. What is one thing you are really proud of?
  7. Which friend knows you better than anyone else?
  8. What's the weirdest habit you have?
  9. What is a skill you would love to learn?
  10. What's one thing that always makes you laugh?

Popular Dare Questions

  1. Do a 15-second dance without music.
  2. Speak in a robot voice until your next turn.
  3. Tell a joke until someone laughs.
  4. Act like a news reporter and interview another player.
  5. Walk around the room like a penguin for 30 seconds.
  6. Sing the chorus of your favorite song.
  7. Give everyone in the group a funny nickname.
  8. Keep a serious face while everyone tries to make you laugh.
  9. Make up a short rap about someone in the group.
  10. Pretend the floor is lava for 30 seconds.

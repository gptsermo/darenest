Truth or Dare Questions for Teens

Explore these questions and enjoy a fun game with your group. Choose a question, take turns and keep the game entertaining for everyone.

  1. What's the funniest thing that has happened in your classroom?
  2. Who is the funniest person in your friend group?
  3. What's your favorite subject?
  4. What's the strangest excuse you've ever used for not doing homework?
  5. Have you ever fallen asleep during class?
  6. What's your favorite school memory?
  7. If you could change one school rule, what would it be?
  8. What's the funniest nickname you've ever had?
  9. Which teacher would you choose to be your teammate on a game show?
  10. What's your favorite thing to do after school?
  11. Have you ever forgotten about an important assignment?
  12. If you could add one new subject to school, what would it be?
  13. What's the funniest thing you've seen happen during a school event?
  14. Which school activity would you like to try?
  15. What's your favorite school lunch or snack?
  16. If your school were a movie, what would its title be?
  17. What's one skill you wish you could learn instantly?
  18. Who would be most likely to become famous in your friend group?
  19. What's your funniest school-day mistake?
  20. What's one thing you actually enjoy about school?
  21. Pretend you're a teacher giving a lesson about something completely silly.
  22. Give a 30-second speech about why homework should disappear.
  23. Act like you're arriving late to class.
  24. Pretend you're the principal announcing a ridiculous new school rule.
  25. Create a funny name for an imaginary school.
  26. Give a dramatic presentation about your favorite snack.
  27. Pretend you're accepting an award for "Student of the Year."
  28. Act like you're a sports commentator describing a school game.
  29. Invent a new school subject and explain what students would learn.
  30. Pretend you're a news reporter covering the school cafeteria.
  31. Give your best motivational speech about surviving Monday morning.
  32. Create a funny school mascot and demonstrate its celebration.
  33. Pretend you're trying to convince a teacher to give the class an extra break.
  34. Describe your school day like it's an action movie trailer.
  35. Make up a 20-second advertisement for your imaginary school.
  36. Pretend you're hosting a school talent show.
  37. Give another player a funny but positive school award.
  38. Act like you're giving a graduation speech.
  39. Invent a ridiculous school club and explain what members do.
  40. Finish with your best "last day of school" celebration.

How to Play

Take turns choosing a question and answer honestly or complete the challenge when appropriate. Keep the game respectful and comfortable for everyone.

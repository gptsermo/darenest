Truth or Dare Questions for Siblings

Explore these questions and enjoy a fun game with your group. Choose a question, take turns and keep the game entertaining for everyone.

  1. What's the funniest memory you have with your sibling?
  2. Have you ever blamed your sibling for something you did?
  3. Who was more mischievous as a child?
  4. What's the funniest nickname you've given your sibling?
  5. What's one thing your sibling does that annoys you?
  6. Which sibling would survive longest on a deserted island?
  7. Have you ever secretly taken something belonging to your sibling?
  8. What's the most ridiculous argument you've had?
  9. Who is more likely to become famous?
  10. What's one thing your sibling is better at than you?
  11. What's your favorite childhood memory together?
  12. Have you ever laughed together at a completely inappropriate moment?
  13. If you started a business together, what would it be?
  14. Which sibling is more likely to spend money impulsively?
  15. What's the funniest thing your sibling has ever said?
  16. If your sibling were a movie character, who would they be?
  17. What's one adventure you'd like to have together?
  18. Who would be better at surviving without a phone for a week?
  19. What's one thing you genuinely admire about your sibling?
  20. If your sibling could read your mind for one day, what would you worry they might discover?
  21. Recreate a funny childhood memory together.
  22. Create a secret sibling handshake.
  23. Give your sibling three genuine compliments.
  24. Pretend you're both famous celebrities being interviewed.
  25. Create a ridiculous business you could run together.
  26. Take the funniest sibling selfie you can.
  27. Act out how you used to argue as children.
  28. Invent superhero names for each other.
  29. Create a two-person dance routine.
  30. Pretend you're lost together on a road trip.
  31. Give your sibling a dramatic movie-style introduction.
  32. Tell a story about your childhood using exactly five words.
  33. Pretend you're accepting an award for "Best Sibling Team."
  34. Create a funny advertisement for your sibling relationship.
  35. Try to make each other laugh without speaking.
  36. Pretend you're contestants on a family game show.
  37. Invent a ridiculous family tradition.
  38. Give a 20-second speech about why your sibling is awesome.
  39. Create a funny team name for the two of you.

How to Play

Take turns choosing a question and answer honestly or complete the challenge when appropriate. Keep the game respectful and comfortable for everyone.

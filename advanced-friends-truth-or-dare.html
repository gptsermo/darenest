Advanced Truth or Dare Questions for Friends

Explore these questions and enjoy a fun game with your group. Choose a question, take turns and keep the game entertaining for everyone.

  1. Which friend has influenced your life the most?
  2. What's one thing you've never apologized for but should have?
  3. If you could relive one day with your friends, which day would it be?
  4. Have you ever been jealous of one of your friends?
  5. What's the kindest thing a friend has ever done for you?
  6. Which friend would you trust to keep your biggest secret forever?
  7. What's one funny rumor you've heard about yourself?
  8. Have you ever accidentally revealed someone else's secret?
  9. If your friends could change one thing about you, what do you think it would be?
  10. What's the biggest lesson a friendship has taught you?
  11. Which friend would you take on a road trip and why?
  12. Have you ever laughed during a serious moment with friends?
  13. What's the most unforgettable gift you've received from a friend?
  14. Which friend would make the best detective?
  15. If your group had a leader, who would it be?
  16. What's one habit of yours that annoys your friends?
  17. Which friend is most likely to become famous?
  18. Have you ever judged someone before knowing them?
  19. What's one challenge you've overcome with the help of friends?
  20. If today were your last day with this group, what would you say to them?
  21. Speak only in questions until your next turn.
  22. Let another player choose a funny profile picture for your phone for 10 minutes.
  23. Tell a story using only five words.
  24. Pretend you're a tour guide showing everyone around the room.
  25. Try to juggle three safe objects for 30 seconds.
  26. Walk like your favorite animal until your next turn.
  27. Make up a slogan for your friend group.
  28. Compliment every player using only one word each.
  29. Pretend you're an alien visiting Earth for the first time.
  30. Balance a coin on your forehead for 20 seconds.
  31. Do your best slow-motion walk across the room.
  32. Act like you're competing in a cooking show for one minute.
  33. Try to whistle your favorite song.
  34. Invent a new handshake with the player on your left.
  35. Narrate everything you do for the next minute like a sports commentator.
  36. Freeze in place until someone says your name.
  37. Draw an emoji with your eyes closed and let everyone guess it.
  38. Pretend your shoe is a phone and have a 30-second conversation.
  39. Make up a superhero name for every player in the group.
  40. End your turn with your biggest, funniest victory pose

How to Play

Take turns choosing a question and answer honestly or complete the challenge when appropriate. Keep the game respectful and comfortable for everyone.

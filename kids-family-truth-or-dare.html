Truth or Dare Questions for Kids and Family

Explore these questions and enjoy a fun game with your group. Choose a question, take turns and keep the game entertaining for everyone.

  1. What's your favorite cartoon?
  2. What is your favorite game to play with friends?
  3. What's the funniest thing you've ever done at school?
  4. If you could have any animal as a pet, what would you choose?
  5. What's your favorite ice cream flavor?
  6. If you could have one superpower, what would it be?
  7. What's your favorite family activity?
  8. Who makes you laugh the most?
  9. What's the funniest nickname you've ever had?
  10. If you could visit any place in the world, where would you go?
  11. What's your favorite movie?
  12. What's the best birthday you've ever had?
  13. If you could eat one food every day for a week, what would it be?
  14. What's your favorite thing to do on a rainy day?
  15. If you could become any animal for a day, which one would you choose?
  16. What's the funniest joke you know?
  17. Who is your favorite fictional character?
  18. What's one thing you're really good at?
  19. If you could invent a new game, what would it be?
  20. What's one thing that always makes you happy?
  21. Do your funniest dance for 20 seconds.
  22. Make your best animal sound and let everyone guess it.
  23. Pretend you're a superhero for 30 seconds.
  24. Walk like a penguin across the room.
  25. Sing your favorite song.
  26. Make your funniest facial expression.
  27. Pretend you're a robot for one minute.
  28. Act like your favorite cartoon character.
  29. Tell a funny joke.
  30. Draw a picture with your eyes closed.
  31. Balance a book on your head for 20 seconds.
  32. Pretend you're a news reporter and report what's happening in the room.
  33. Make up a silly song about your family.
  34. Act like a monkey for 20 seconds.
  35. Try to say the alphabet backwards.
  36. Pretend the floor is lava for 30 seconds.
  37. Create a new dance move and give it a funny name.
  38. Make three different animal sounds.
  39. Pretend you're a chef explaining how to make your favorite meal.
  40. Give everyone a high-five and say something nice about them.

How to Play

Take turns choosing a question and answer honestly or complete the challenge when appropriate. Keep the game respectful and comfortable for everyone.

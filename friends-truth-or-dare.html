Truth Questions for Friends

Ask these fun Truth questions and learn something surprising about your friends.

  1. Who in this group do you trust the most?
  2. What's the biggest lie you've ever told a friend?
  3. Who was your first best friend?
  4. Have you ever secretly disliked one of your friends?
  5. What's the most embarrassing thing you've done in front of friends?
  6. Who in this group would survive a zombie apocalypse?
  7. Have you ever ignored a friend's message on purpose?
  8. What's one secret you've never told your friends?
  9. Which friend knows you better than anyone else?
  10. Have you ever borrowed something and never returned it?
  11. What's the funniest prank you've ever played?
  12. Who in this group would make the best actor?
  13. Have you ever pretended to be sick to avoid meeting friends?
  14. What's your biggest fear that your friends don't know about?
  15. If you had to swap lives with one friend, who would it be?
  16. What's the most childish thing you still do?
  17. Have you ever laughed at the wrong moment?
  18. What's the weirdest habit you have?
  19. Who in this group would you trust with your biggest secret?
  20. What's one thing you're glad your friends don't know about you?

Dare Questions for Friends

Ready for a challenge? Try these fun and entertaining Dares.

  1. Challenge another player to a 15-second dance-off and copy each other's moves.
  2. Call your best friend and say, "I have amazing news!" then reveal it was a dare.
  3. Speak in a robot voice until your next turn.
  4. Balance a book on your head for one minute.
  5. Sing the chorus of your favorite song loudly.
  6. Tell a joke until someone laughs.
  7. Pretend to be a news reporter and interview another player.
  8. Walk around the room like a penguin for one minute.
  9. Do 15 jumping jacks.
  10. Try to lick your elbow for 20 seconds.
  11. Say the alphabet backwards as far as you can.
  12. Wear your shirt or cap backwards for the next three rounds.
  13. Act like your favorite movie character for one minute.
  14. Give everyone in the group a funny nickname.
  15. Keep a serious face while everyone tries to make you laugh for 30 seconds.
  16. Talk without using the letter "A" for one minute.
  17. Ask another player to give you a funny nickname and use it for the next 3 rounds.
  18. Imitate a celebrity until someone guesses who it is.
  19. Pretend the floor is lava for the next 30 seconds.
  20. Make up a short rap about one of your friends.

How to Play Truth or Dare with Friends

Gather your friends, choose a player and take turns choosing Truth or Dare. Answer the Truth honestly or complete the Dare before moving to the next player.

Truth Questions for Office

Ask these light-hearted Truth questions and have some fun with your coworkers while keeping the game workplace-friendly.

  1. Have you ever pretended to be busy when your boss walked by?
  2. Who is the funniest person in your office?
  3. Have you ever arrived late and blamed the traffic?
  4. What's your favorite part of your job?
  5. If you could swap jobs with one colleague for a day, who would it be?
  6. Have you ever forgotten an important meeting?
  7. Who makes the best tea or coffee in the office?
  8. What's the funniest thing that has happened during a meeting?
  9. Have you ever sent a message to the wrong person?
  10. What's one office habit you wish people would stop doing?
  11. If your office had a talent show, what would you perform?
  12. Which colleague would survive best on a deserted island?
  13. Have you ever laughed during a serious meeting?
  14. What's your dream office perk?
  15. If you became the CEO for one day, what's the first change you'd make?
  16. What's the best compliment you've received at work?
  17. What's one skill you'd like to improve this year?
  18. Which colleague would make the best stand-up comedian?
  19. If your office became a movie, what would its title be?
  20. What's one thing you enjoy most about working with your team?

Dare Questions for Office

Ready for a fun workplace challenge? Try these entertaining Dares with your team.

  1. Give a genuine compliment to a colleague.
  2. Organize your desk in under one minute.
  3. Speak in a motivational speaker's voice for your next sentence.
  4. Stand up and stretch for 30 seconds.
  5. Pretend you're presenting the Employee of the Year award.
  6. Walk to the water dispenser in slow motion.
  7. Tell a clean office joke.
  8. Introduce yourself as if it's your first day at work.
  9. Make a paper airplane and see how far it flies, if allowed.
  10. Say three positive things about your workplace.
  11. Pretend you're answering an imaginary customer call.
  12. Smile continuously for the next minute.
  13. Act like the office printer when it jams.
  14. Share one productivity tip with the group.
  15. Balance a pen on one finger for 15 seconds.
  16. Describe your job using only five words.
  17. Let another player choose a word you must use three times in your next conversation.
  18. Imitate the office coffee machine or photocopier.
  19. Thank someone at work for something they've helped you with.
  20. Create a funny slogan for your office team.

How to Play Truth or Dare at the Office

Take turns choosing Truth or Dare. Answer the Truth honestly or complete the Dare before moving to the next player.

Keep the game respectful and comfortable for everyone. DareNest makes it easy to start playing with questions designed for different groups and occasions.

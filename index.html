How to Play Truth or Dare

Playing is simple: choose your group, age and game mode, then start a challenge. Answer a Truth honestly or complete a Dare, and use Next Challenge to keep the game going.

  1. Choose your group or category.
  2. Select an age group.
  3. Choose Truth, Dare or another available game.
  4. Answer the question or complete the challenge.
  5. Tap Next Challenge and keep playing.

DareNest is designed to work directly in your browser, so you can play on a phone, tablet or computer without an account.

Truth or Dare FAQ

What is Truth or Dare?

Truth or Dare is a group party game where players choose between answering a question honestly or completing a fun challenge.

Is DareNest free?

Yes. DareNest lets you play its online question and challenge games for free.

Can I play Truth or Dare online with friends?

Yes. Open DareNest on a phone, tablet or computer and take turns with the people in your group.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. DareNest is designed for mobile and desktop browsers, so you can play without installing an app.

How does the random generator work?

Choose your category, age group and game mode. DareNest then selects a matching challenge from its question bank.

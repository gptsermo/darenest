Truth or Dare Questions for Friends in the USA

Explore these questions and enjoy a fun game with your group. Choose a question, take turns and keep the game entertaining for everyone.

  1. Which fast-food chain could you eat at every week?
  2. What's your favorite American holiday: Thanksgiving, Halloween, or the 4th of July?
  3. Have you ever skipped work or school because you stayed up too late?
  4. What's the most embarrassing thing you've done at a school or college party?
  5. If you could take a road trip across any three U.S. states, which ones would you choose?
  6. What's your favorite American TV show or sitcom?
  7. Have you ever gotten a speeding ticket?
  8. Which U.S. city would you most like to visit?
  9. What's the strangest thing you've ever ordered from a drive-thru?
  10. If you suddenly received $1,000, what would you spend it on?
  11. Have you ever pretended to understand an American sports reference when you didn't?
  12. Which sport would you choose to play professionally if you had the talent?
  13. What's your favorite Halloween costume you've ever worn?
  14. Have you ever gotten lost on a road trip?
  15. If you could live in any U.S. state for one year, where would you live?
  16. What's the most American thing you've ever done?
  17. Which would you rather attend: the Super Bowl, NBA Finals, or World Series?
  18. Have you ever eaten an entire pizza by yourself over more than one sitting?
  19. What's your favorite type of American comfort food?
  20. If you could spend a weekend in New York City, Los Angeles, Miami, or Las Vegas, which would you pick?
  21. Pretend you're announcing the Super Bowl for 30 seconds.
  22. Give a dramatic weather forecast for the room.
  23. Pretend you're ordering your dream meal at a drive-thru.
  24. Do your best touchdown celebration.
  25. Give a 20-second acceptance speech for winning an imaginary award.
  26. Pretend you're a Hollywood movie trailer narrator.
  27. Act like you're giving a tour of your hometown to a tourist.
  28. Create a funny commercial for your favorite fast-food restaurant.
  29. Pretend you're a baseball commentator describing someone's actions.
  30. Do your best impression of someone giving a Thanksgiving dinner speech.
  31. Give a motivational speech about something completely ordinary, like doing laundry.
  32. Pretend you're a game-show host and introduce the next player.
  33. Act out your reaction to winning $1 million.
  34. Create a 15-second advertisement for an imaginary American product.
  35. Pretend you're a traffic reporter describing an imaginary traffic jam.
  36. Sing the first few lines of a song everyone in the group might know.
  37. Give your best "Welcome to Hollywood!" announcement.
  38. Pretend you're a sports coach giving your team a halftime speech.
  39. Invent a ridiculous new American holiday and explain how people celebrate it.
  40. Give a dramatic speech about why your favorite snack deserves to be America's official snack.

How to Play

Take turns choosing a question and answer honestly or complete the challenge when appropriate. Keep the game respectful and comfortable for everyone.

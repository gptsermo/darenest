🎭 Two Truths and a Lie

Choose the statement you think is the lie, reveal the answer, and keep the round moving.

Round 1

Which statement is the lie?

Choose one statement first.

How to Play Two Truths and a Lie

  1. Read the three statements.
  2. Everyone guesses which statement is the lie.
  3. Tap your choice and reveal the answer.
  4. Tap Next Round and keep playing.

Use it as a quick icebreaker at parties, with friends, at the office, or with family.

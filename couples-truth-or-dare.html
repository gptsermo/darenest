Truth Questions for Couples

Ask these Truth questions to learn more about your partner and spark interesting conversations.

  1. What was your first impression of your partner?
  2. Who said "I love you" first?
  3. What's your favorite memory together?
  4. What's one habit of your partner that secretly makes you smile?
  5. What's the sweetest surprise you've ever received?
  6. What's one thing you've always wanted to tell your partner but haven't?
  7. If you could relive one date together, which one would it be?
  8. What's your partner's cutest habit?
  9. Have you ever forgotten an important date or anniversary?
  10. What quality do you admire most in your partner?
  11. If you could travel anywhere together, where would you go?
  12. What's one thing your partner does better than you?
  13. What's the funniest argument you've ever had?
  14. What song reminds you of your relationship?
  15. If your partner won a million dollars, what do you think they'd buy first?
  16. What's one small thing your partner does that makes your day better?
  17. What's your happiest moment together so far?
  18. If you had to describe your relationship in one word, what would it be?
  19. What is one dream you want to achieve together?
  20. What's one thing you appreciate about your partner that you don't say often?

Dare Questions for Couples

Ready for a fun challenge? Try these Dares with your partner.

  1. Hold hands for the next 2 minutes.
  2. Give your partner three genuine compliments.
  3. Recreate your first photo together.
  4. Dance together for one minute without music.
  5. Look into each other's eyes for 30 seconds without laughing.
  6. Feed your partner a small snack without using your hands.
  7. Tell your partner your favorite memory of them.
  8. Let your partner choose your phone wallpaper for one hour.
  9. Act out how you first met.
  10. Sing a romantic song to your partner.
  11. Give your partner a high-five followed by a hug.

  17. Try to make your partner laugh without touching them.

  19. Exchange one fun nickname and use it until the end of the game.

How to Play Truth or Dare as a Couple

Take turns choosing Truth or Dare. Answer the Truth honestly or complete the Dare before switching turns.

Truth or Dare Questions for College Students

Explore these questions and enjoy a fun game with your group. Choose a question, take turns and keep the game entertaining for everyone.

  1. What's the funniest thing that has happened in one of your classes?
  2. Have you ever pretended to understand a lecture when you were completely lost?
  3. What's the strangest excuse you've used for missing class?
  4. Which subject would you happily never study again?
  5. Have you ever studied all night before an exam?
  6. What's your most embarrassing classroom moment?
  7. Have you ever forgotten about an assignment until the last minute?
  8. Who was your favorite teacher or professor, and why?
  9. What's the funniest nickname you've had at school or college?
  10. Have you ever fallen asleep during a lecture?
  11. What's the most unusual thing you've carried in your backpack?
  12. Have you ever accidentally submitted the wrong file?
  13. If you could change one school or college rule, what would it be?
  14. What's your favorite memory with your classmates?
  15. Have you ever pretended to be sick to avoid an exam?
  16. Which class would you take just because it sounds interesting?
  17. What's the funniest thing you've seen happen during an exam?
  18. If you could swap lives with one classmate for a day, who would it be?
  19. What's one skill you wish schools taught everyone?
  20. If your student life were a movie, what would its title be?
  21. Give a 30-second lecture about something completely ridiculous.
  22. Pretend you're a professor teaching an imaginary subject.
  23. Act like you're giving a presentation while everything goes hilariously wrong.
  24. Explain your favorite food as if you're defending a thesis.
  25. Pretend you're answering a very difficult exam question.
  26. Give a motivational speech about surviving exam week.
  27. Act out a teacher taking attendance.
  28. Create a ridiculous name for an imaginary college.
  29. Pretend you're the principal announcing a completely absurd new rule.
  30. Explain how to make a sandwich as if you're teaching a university course.
  31. Give a dramatic presentation about why weekends should be longer.
  32. Pretend you're accepting an award for "Student of the Century."
  33. Act like you're trying to convince a professor to give you one extra point.
  34. Create a 20-second advertisement for your imaginary college.
  35. Explain your daily routine without using the words "school," "college," or "class."
  36. Pretend you're a news reporter covering exam week.
  37. Give a dramatic farewell speech to your imaginary final exam.
  38. Invent a new subject and explain what students would learn.
  39. Act like you're a student arriving five minutes late to an important exam.
  40. Give your best "I definitely studied for this" performance.

How to Play

Take turns choosing a question and answer honestly or complete the challenge when appropriate. Keep the game respectful and comfortable for everyone.

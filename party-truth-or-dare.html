Truth Questions for Parties

Ask these fun Truth questions and get the party started with surprising answers and plenty of laughs.

  1. Who at this party would survive the longest on a deserted island?
  2. What's the craziest thing you've ever done at a party?
  3. Have you ever accidentally embarrassed yourself in public?
  4. What's your most unforgettable party memory?
  5. Who here do you think has the best sense of humor?
  6. Have you ever danced when no one else was dancing?
  7. What's the funniest excuse you've ever used to leave a party?
  8. If you could invite any celebrity to this party, who would it be?
  9. Have you ever laughed so hard that you cried?
  10. What's the weirdest food you've ever eaten?
  11. Who here do you think would win a comedy competition?
  12. What's the funniest nickname you've ever had?
  13. Have you ever sung karaoke in front of a crowd?
  14. If you had one superpower for tonight, what would it be?
  15. What's one hidden talent most people at this party don't know about?
  16. If you could swap lives with someone here for one day, who would it be?
  17. Have you ever accidentally called someone by the wrong name?
  18. What's the most adventurous thing you've ever done?
  19. What's one thing on your bucket list you hope to do someday?
  20. If tonight became a movie, what would its title be?

Dare Questions for Parties

Ready for a challenge? Try these fun and entertaining Dares with your friends and party group.

  1. Dance continuously for one minute.
  2. Sing the chorus of your favorite song as dramatically as possible.
  3. Pretend you're the DJ and announce the next imaginary song.
  4. Tell your funniest joke.
  5. Do your best robot dance for 30 seconds.
  6. Pretend you are a famous singer and perform the chorus of any song for 20 seconds.
  7. Let another player choose your pose for a group photo.
  8. Speak only in rhymes until your next turn.
  9. Pretend you're walking on the moon for one minute.
  10. Make three different animal sounds and let everyone guess them.
  11. Wear your jacket or cap backwards until your next turn.
  12. Try to keep a straight face while everyone tries to make you laugh.
  13. Introduce yourself as if you're a superhero.
  14. Act like you're accepting an Oscar award.
  15. Do 10 squats while counting loudly.
  16. Create a funny handshake with the player next to you.
  17. Imitate a famous movie character until someone guesses who it is.
  18. Pretend to be a sports commentator describing what's happening in the room.
  19. Tell a two-line story where every sentence must start with the next letter of the alphabet.
  20. Give everyone in the group a high-five and end with your biggest smile.

How to Play Truth or Dare at a Party

Gather your group, choose a player and take turns choosing Truth or Dare. Answer the Truth honestly or complete the Dare before moving to the next player.

DareNest makes it easy to start playing instantly with questions for different groups and occasions.

Truth or Dare Questions for Couples

Explore these questions and enjoy a fun game with your group. Choose a question, take turns and keep the game entertaining for everyone.

  1. If we could take a road trip anywhere in the U.S., where would you want to go?
  2. Which U.S. city would be your dream destination for a couple's weekend?
  3. Would you rather spend Valentine's Day in New York City or on a beach in Miami?
  4. What's your ideal American-style date night?
  5. Which movie would you want us to watch together on a Friday night?
  6. If we won a $10,000 vacation package, where would you choose to go?
  7. Would you rather attend a Super Bowl party or a romantic dinner?
  8. Which American holiday would you most enjoy celebrating together?
  9. What's your favorite comfort food for a cozy night together?
  10. If we could live in any U.S. state for a year, which would you choose?
  11. Would you rather explore Disneyland or Universal Studios together?
  12. What's one U.S. landmark you'd love to visit with your partner?
  13. If we had a free weekend in Las Vegas, what would you want to do?
  14. What's your favorite American TV couple?
  15. Would you rather have a picnic in Central Park or watch a sunset on a California beach?
  16. If we opened a small business together, what would it be?
  17. What's one American city you think would suit our personalities?
  18. Would you rather have a Thanksgiving dinner together or a Fourth of July barbecue?
  19. If we could attend any major U.S. music festival together, which would you pick?
  20. What's one American experience you'd love for us to try together?
  21. Plan your dream U.S. road trip together in 60 seconds.
  22. Pretend you're proposing to your partner in a Hollywood movie.
  23. Give your partner a dramatic "movie trailer" introduction.
  24. Pretend you're ordering your partner's dream meal at a drive-thru.
  25. Create a 20-second commercial advertising your relationship.
  26. Act out your first date as if it were a scene from a sitcom.
  27. Give your partner a dramatic Valentine's Day speech.
  28. Pretend you're contestants on an American game show.
  29. Create a funny couple nickname inspired by an American city.
  30. Give your partner three compliments using exaggerated Hollywood-style drama.
  31. Pretend you're planning a surprise weekend in New York City.
  32. Act like you're a sports commentator describing your partner entering the room.
  33. Create a fictional restaurant and invent your couple's signature dish.
  34. Pretend you're accepting an award for "Best Couple of the Year."
  35. Give a 30-second speech about why your partner deserves a million-dollar prize.
  36. Pretend you're competing together on a cooking show.
  37. Create a funny advertisement for your dream vacation together.
  38. Recreate a famous romantic movie scene using your own dialogue.
  39. Pretend you're announcing your relationship at a huge stadium.
  40. Plan your imaginary dream wedding in exactly five sentences.

How to Play

Take turns choosing a question and answer honestly or complete the challenge when appropriate. Keep the game respectful and comfortable for everyone.

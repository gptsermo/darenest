Pet Truth or Dare Questions

Explore these questions and enjoy a fun game with your group. Choose a question, take turns and keep the game entertaining for everyone.

  18. What's one animal you'd love to see in the wild?
  19. Would you rather have a pet that can talk or one that can sing?
  20. If you could create a new animal, what would it look like?

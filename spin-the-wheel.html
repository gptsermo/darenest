How Spin the Wheel Works

  1. Choose who you are playing with and the age group.
  2. DareNest loads matching questions and challenges from its game collection.
  3. Tap Spin the Wheel and watch the wheel animate.
  4. The wheel stops on a random position and reveals your challenge.
  5. Tap Spin Again whenever you want another challenge.

The wheel works directly in your browser and is designed for phones, tablets and computers.

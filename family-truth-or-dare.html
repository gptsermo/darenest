Truth Questions for Family

Ask these fun Truth questions and share laughs and interesting stories with your family.

  1. Who in your family makes you laugh the most?
  2. Who is the best cook in your family?
  3. What is your funniest childhood memory?
  4. Have you ever blamed a sibling for something you did?
  5. Which family member gives the best advice?
  6. What's one family tradition you love?
  7. Have you ever hidden your report card or exam results?
  8. Who is the funniest person in your family?
  9. What's your favorite family vacation memory?
  10. Have you ever secretly eaten someone else's food at home?
  11. Which family member do you call first when you need help?
  12. What's one funny nickname you have in your family?
  13. Have you ever broken something at home and blamed someone else?
  14. What's your favorite homemade meal?
  15. Which family member do you look up to the most?
  16. What's the funniest thing your parents have ever said?
  17. If your family had a reality TV show, what would it be called?
  18. What's one habit you inherited from your parents?
  19. Who in your family would survive longest on a deserted island?
  20. What's one thing you appreciate most about your family?

Dare Questions for Family

Ready for a fun family challenge? Try these entertaining Dares.

  1. Give every family member a genuine compliment.
  2. Act like your mom or dad until someone guesses who you are.
  3. Sing your favorite childhood rhyme.
  4. Tell a funny family story.
  5. Help clean up for the next 5 minutes.
  6. Call a grandparent or relative and say hello.
  7. Do your funniest dance for 30 seconds.
  8. Speak in a cartoon character's voice for one minute.
  9. Hug every family member in the room.
  10. Draw a funny picture of your family in one minute.
  11. Act like a news reporter and give a 20-second funny news report about something happening in the room.
  12. Say three things you're thankful for about your family.
  13. Pretend to be the oldest person in the family for one minute.
  14. Walk backward until your next turn.
  15. Tell everyone your funniest childhood nickname.
  16. Make your best animal sound and let everyone guess the animal.
  17. Let another player choose a funny pose for you to hold for 20 seconds.
  18. Say the alphabet without using the letter "E" (stop when you can't continue).
  19. Share one happy family memory.
  20. Give someone in your family a high-five and tell them why they're awesome.

How to Play Truth or Dare with Family

Take turns choosing Truth or Dare. Answer the Truth honestly or complete the Dare before switching to the next player.

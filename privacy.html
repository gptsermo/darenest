Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

DareNest respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use our website and how we use it.

Information we collect

DareNest does not require an account to play its games. We do not intentionally ask visitors to provide sensitive personal information.

We may receive technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location, pages visited and usage events through analytics or advertising technologies when those services are enabled.

Google Analytics

DareNest uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website and to improve performance and content. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to collect usage information.

Cookies and advertising

DareNest may use cookies and similar technologies for analytics, site functionality and, when advertising is enabled, advertising measurement and delivery.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, third parties may place and read cookies or use web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to provide, measure or personalize advertising as permitted by applicable law and the relevant platform policies.

Where required, DareNest will provide appropriate consent choices and will configure advertising for child-directed or age-restricted areas in accordance with applicable requirements.

How Google may use data

When Google services are used on DareNest, Google may process information in accordance with its own policies. For information about how Google uses data when you use partner sites and apps, see How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

Local storage and saved favorites

DareNest may store favorite questions or game preferences locally in your browser. This local data is intended to improve your experience and is not an account or server-side profile.

Children and family content

Some DareNest pages are designed for family or younger audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and analytics on child-directed content will be configured to follow applicable laws and platform policies.

Third-party services

Links or services provided by third parties are governed by their own privacy policies. DareNest is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as DareNest adds features, analytics or advertising services. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Why Use a Random Group Generator?

Making random teams by hand can be awkward when everyone is standing around waiting. DareNest quickly distributes names into groups so you can start your office activity, party game, college event or family activity.

How It Works

  1. Enter each person's name on a separate line.
  2. Choose how many groups you want.
  3. Tap Make Random Groups.
  4. Use Shuffle Again if you want a different arrangement.

If the number of people does not divide evenly, DareNest distributes the extra people across the first groups so the group sizes differ by no more than one.
